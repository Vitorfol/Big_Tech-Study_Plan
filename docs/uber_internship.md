## Banco de Perguntas para o Entrevistador (Referência Rápida)

1. **AI vs. Craft:**

   > *"I really enjoy the craft of writing and debugging code from scratch, though obviously AI tools are becoming a huge and necessary part of how we ship software today. At Uber, how do you personally balance using AI to accelerate delivery while still keeping that hands-on engineering satisfaction we used to have before AI?"*

2. **Scale & Motivation:**

   > *"For me, working on challenging, high-scale problems like Uber's has been a big career goal. Once you've been at Uber for a while and gotten used to that scale, what are the technical problems or engineering challenges that still keep you excited day to day?"*

3. **Intern Habits (Plano C / Tempo curto):**

   > *"Looking back at interns or junior engineers who really stood out on your team, what habits or technical strengths made the biggest difference in their first few months? I believe proactivity and a willingness to learn are just as important as technical skills early in a career, though I'd love to hear your take on that."*
